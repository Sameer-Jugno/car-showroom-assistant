# 1. Model Overview & History

## Introduction

The **Honda Amaze VX i-DTEC** is a compact four-door sedan developed by Honda Motor Company and positioned in the subcompact (B-segment) sedan category. The **VX i-DTEC** represents one of the higher trim levels equipped with Honda's 1.5-liter i-DTEC turbo diesel engine, offering a combination of excellent fuel efficiency, practical interior space, modern features, and low long-term operating costs.

The Honda Amaze was developed primarily for emerging markets where buyers demanded a sedan that combined:

* Compact exterior dimensions suitable for congested cities.
* Spacious passenger accommodation.
* Large luggage capacity.
* Excellent fuel economy.
* Reliable engineering.
* Affordable ownership.
* Modern styling.
* Comfortable ride quality.

Unlike larger Honda sedans such as the City or Civic, the Amaze focuses on maximizing value while retaining Honda's reputation for engineering quality and reliability.

In Pakistan, the Honda Amaze was **never officially assembled or marketed by Honda Atlas Cars Pakistan**. Consequently, all examples found in the country are imported used vehicles, usually sourced from Japan (where applicable), India, or other regional markets through private import channels. Although relatively uncommon compared with the locally available Honda City or Toyota Yaris, the Amaze appeals to buyers seeking an imported diesel sedan with low running costs and a generous equipment level.

The **VX i-DTEC** variant is particularly attractive for:

* Daily commuters with high annual mileage.
* Small families.
* Professionals.
* Ride-hailing operators (where diesel vehicles are practical).
* Buyers upgrading from hatchbacks.
* Owners seeking better fuel economy than petrol sedans.
* Long-distance highway travelers.

# Historical Background

## Origins of the Honda Amaze

The Honda Amaze was introduced as Honda's first modern compact sedan specifically designed for emerging markets. Rather than adapting an existing global sedan, Honda created the Amaze to meet regional demands for:

* Affordable pricing.
* Low ownership costs.
* Compact dimensions.
* Practical family transportation.
* Strong fuel economy.
* Spacious cabin.

The Amaze shares much of its engineering foundation with the Honda Brio hatchback but incorporates a separate three-box sedan design with a significantly larger luggage compartment.

Its development reflected changing customer preferences, particularly in South Asia, where compact sedans became increasingly popular among buyers seeking the prestige and practicality of a sedan without the size and expense of larger C-segment models.

---

## Launch in the Indian Market

Honda introduced the Amaze in **2013** in India, marking several important milestones for the company.

Notably, it was:

* Honda's first diesel passenger car launched in India.
* The first Honda sedan based on the Brio platform.
* One of the earliest compact sedans designed specifically to benefit from local tax regulations favoring vehicles under four meters in length.

The Amaze quickly gained popularity due to its:

* Honda brand reputation.
* Efficient diesel engine.
* Comfortable ride.
* Competitive pricing.
* Spacious interior.
* Large trunk.
* Low maintenance requirements.

It became one of Honda's highest-selling models in several Asian markets.

## The i-DTEC Diesel Engine

The defining feature of this variant is Honda's **1.5-liter i-DTEC turbocharged diesel engine**.

The i-DTEC engine was developed to provide:

* Excellent fuel efficiency.
* High torque output.
* Low emissions.
* Quiet operation for a diesel engine.
* Strong long-distance performance.
* Reliable daily usability.

Honda engineered the i-DTEC to compete directly with diesel offerings from manufacturers such as Hyundai, Maruti Suzuki, Fiat, Toyota, and Volkswagen.

The engine quickly gained recognition for delivering some of the best fuel economy figures in its segment.

# Diesel Fuel Availability in Pakistan

The Honda Amaze VX i-DTEC uses **high-speed diesel (HSD)**, which is readily available across Pakistan through major fuel retailers such as Pakistan State Oil (PSO), Shell, Attock Petroleum, GO Petroleum, and Total PARCO. Diesel availability is generally consistent in cities and along major intercity highways.

To maintain the health of the common-rail fuel injection system, owners should:

* Refuel from reputable fuel stations.
* Replace the diesel fuel filter at recommended intervals.
* Avoid contaminated or low-quality diesel.
* Drain water from the fuel system if contamination is suspected.
* Follow recommended engine oil specifications suitable for modern turbocharged diesel engines.

The Honda Amaze VX i-DTEC has developed a strong reputation across Asian markets for combining Honda's engineering standards with impressive diesel efficiency and practical everyday usability. When serviced according to schedule and fueled with good-quality diesel, the i-DTEC engine is capable of delivering dependable long-term performance and low operating costs.

* Large luggage compartment suitable for family travel.

As with any imported used vehicle in Pakistan, overall ownership experience depends heavily on the condition of the individual car rather than the model alone. For buyers seeking an efficient, comfortable, and well-equipped imported compact diesel sedan with Honda's reputation for reliability, the Honda Amaze VX i-DTEC remains a practical and compelling option in Pakistan's used vehicle market.
