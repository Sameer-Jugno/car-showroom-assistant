# Customer Reviews & Ownership Testimonials — Honda Amaze VX i-DTEC (Pakistan Used Car Market)

---

## Review 1 — Daily office commute, Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I've owned my Honda Amaze VX i-DTEC for just over three years, and it has been one of the most economical cars I've driven. My daily commute between DHA and Gulberg involves plenty of stop-and-go traffic, but the diesel engine still returns excellent fuel economy. Even with diesel prices fluctuating in Pakistan, my monthly fuel expenses are noticeably lower than when I owned a petrol Honda City.

The VX trim feels well-equipped for a compact sedan. The automatic climate control, steering-mounted controls, and comfortable seats make daily driving enjoyable. Maintenance has mostly been routine, and I service it through a workshop that specializes in imported Honda vehicles.

The only challenge has been sourcing a few trim pieces after a parking accident. Mechanical parts have been manageable, but cosmetic components required waiting for imports. Overall, it's reliable, practical, and far more comfortable than I expected.

## Review 2 — Small business owner, Karachi

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

My work requires visiting clients across Karachi every day, so fuel economy was my biggest priority when I bought the Amaze. The i-DTEC engine has definitely delivered. Even in heavy city traffic, I regularly achieve mileage that keeps fuel costs under control despite rising diesel prices.

The car feels surprisingly refined for a compact sedan. Cabin space is good enough for my family, and the trunk easily handles business samples and luggage. The suspension also absorbs rough roads better than I anticipated.

My only complaint is that because the Amaze was never officially sold here, finding certain electronic or body parts can take time. Routine servicing has been straightforward, but I wouldn't recommend skipping maintenance because modern diesel engines require proper care.

## Review 3 — Family car, 4 years ownership — Islamabad

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

We bought the Amaze mainly for family use, and it has served us extremely well. My wife appreciates how easy it is to drive and park, while I enjoy the diesel engine's effortless highway performance during trips to Murree and Abbottabad.

The rear seat comfortably accommodates our children, and the boot is much larger than people expect from a compact sedan. Fuel consumption on motorway trips has consistently impressed me, making long journeys much more affordable.

Routine maintenance has been predictable, and our mechanic hasn't encountered any major issues. As long as quality diesel is used and servicing is done on schedule, the car feels very dependable. I would happily purchase another Honda diesel in the future.

## Review 4 — Imported car enthusiast, Rawalpindi

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I deliberately chose the Amaze because I wanted something different from the usual Corolla or City. The Honda build quality is immediately noticeable, and the diesel engine offers excellent low-end torque without sacrificing fuel efficiency.

The VX trim includes enough convenience features that the cabin still feels modern despite the vehicle's age. Long drives are comfortable, and the air conditioning performs well even during hot summer afternoons.

The downside is parts availability. Routine service components are easy to find, but body panels and some electronic modules aren't always stocked locally. That hasn't been a deal breaker, but it's something buyers should understand before purchasing an imported vehicle.

## Review 5 — Second owner, Multan

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

My ownership experience has been mixed. The Amaze itself is an excellent car, but I bought one that hadn't been maintained properly by the previous owner. Within the first six months, I had to replace the clutch, suspension bushes, and repair part of the fuel system.

After investing in those repairs, the vehicle has become dependable. Fuel economy is outstanding, and the diesel engine feels much stronger than I expected from a 1.5-liter sedan. The cabin is comfortable enough for long-distance family trips.

My advice is simple: don't buy based only on appearance. Spend money on a proper inspection because maintenance history matters much more than mileage.

## Review 6 — Government employee, Peshawar

The Amaze has been my daily commuter for almost five years. I drive around 40 kilometers every weekday, and fuel efficiency remains one of its biggest strengths. Compared to my previous petrol sedan, my monthly diesel expenses are significantly lower.

## Review 9 — Ride-hailing and family use, Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I occasionally use my Amaze for ride-hailing during weekends. Passengers often comment that the rear seat is more comfortable than they expected from a compact sedan. The air conditioning cools quickly, and the ride quality is smooth over most city roads.

## Review 10 — Engineer, Abbottabad

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The Amaze performs well in hilly areas because the diesel torque makes climbing much easier than with naturally aspirated petrol cars. I enjoy driving it on mountain roads, and the braking performance has always inspired confidence.

However, I did experience an expensive injector-related repair after unknowingly filling up with poor-quality diesel. Since then, I've only used reputable fuel stations, and the car has been trouble-free.

It's a reminder that modern diesel engines reward good maintenance but can become costly if neglected. Overall, I still consider it a reliable vehicle.

## Review 13 — Second owner, 6 years ownership — Karachi

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I've owned this Amaze longer than any previous vehicle, and it has earned my confidence through consistent reliability. The previous owner maintained it carefully, and I've continued that routine with regular oil changes, filter replacements, and scheduled servicing.

The i-DTEC engine still feels responsive, fuel economy remains exceptional, and highway performance is better than many petrol sedans in the same price range. Even with Pakistan's changing fuel prices, operating costs remain manageable because of the car's efficiency.

Resale demand isn't as strong as locally assembled Honda or Toyota models, but that doesn't concern me because I have no plans to sell it anytime soon. For buyers who value efficiency, practicality, and Honda reliability over brand popularity in the local market, the Amaze VX i-DTEC is an excellent long-term ownership proposition.
