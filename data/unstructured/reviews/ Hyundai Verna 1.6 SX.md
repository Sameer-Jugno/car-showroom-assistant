# Customer Reviews & Ownership Testimonials — Hyundai Verna 1.6 SX Diesel (Pakistan Used Car Market)

---

## Review 1 — Highway commuter, Lahore–Islamabad route

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I travel between Lahore and Islamabad at least twice a month for work, and the Hyundai Verna 1.6 SX Diesel has been one of the most economical long-distance cars I've owned. The 1.6 CRDi engine has plenty of torque for overtaking slow-moving trucks on the motorway, and it cruises comfortably at legal highway speeds without feeling strained.

Fuel economy is the biggest highlight. On long trips, I usually see around 19–21 km/l if I maintain a steady speed, which helps a lot considering diesel prices. The seats are supportive, the cabin remains comfortable over long distances, and the automatic climate control performs well even during Punjab's hot summers.

Maintenance costs are higher than a locally assembled Corolla or City because some parts need to be imported, but routine servicing has been straightforward. Overall, the car has exceeded my expectations for comfort, refinement, and efficiency.

---

## Review 2 — Daily office commute, Karachi

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I use my Verna every weekday in Karachi's heavy traffic, and it has been a dependable companion. The diesel engine produces strong low-end torque, so moving through stop-and-go traffic requires very little effort compared to some petrol cars I've driven.

Fuel economy has remained consistently impressive despite congestion. I switched from a petrol sedan mainly because rising fuel costs were becoming difficult to justify, and this decision has definitely reduced my monthly fuel expenses.

The cabin feels premium for a car of its age, especially the SX trim with its additional features. My only complaint is that replacement electronic components can sometimes be difficult to source locally. Fortunately, my mechanic specializes in imported Hyundais, so servicing hasn't been a major concern.

## Review 3 — Family sedan, owned for 5 years — Rawalpindi

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

The Verna has been our family's primary vehicle for five years, and reliability has been excellent. We've completed several trips to Murree and northern Punjab without experiencing any mechanical issues. The diesel engine feels relaxed on highways and never struggles with four passengers and luggage.

The SX trim includes enough comfort features that the car still feels modern. The rear seat is spacious enough for my children, and the boot easily accommodates family luggage for weekend trips.

Maintenance is slightly more expensive than a Honda City, but fuel savings help balance those additional costs. I also appreciate that the engine remains smooth despite relatively high mileage. If properly maintained, this is an excellent family sedan.

## Review 4 — Imported car enthusiast, Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I specifically wanted something different from the usual Corolla or Civic, and the Verna caught my attention because of its styling and diesel engine. After three years of ownership, I still think it offers excellent value.

Performance is noticeably stronger than I expected from a compact diesel sedan. The turbocharged engine delivers excellent mid-range acceleration, especially when overtaking on highways.

Finding body panels after a small accident was more difficult than sourcing mechanical parts, which was frustrating. However, routine maintenance has been relatively simple because filters, brake pads, and suspension components are available through importers.

If someone wants a comfortable imported sedan with strong fuel economy, I believe the Verna deserves serious consideration.

## Review 5 — Ride-hailing and personal use, Islamabad

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

I originally purchased the Verna for occasional ride-hailing work while also using it for family transportation. Fuel economy has been excellent, and passengers often compliment the comfortable seats and smooth ride.

The downside has been maintenance costs. The diesel injection system requires quality fuel and proper servicing, so I avoid cheaper fuel stations. One injector-related repair was more expensive than I expected, although it hasn't caused problems since.

The vehicle itself is pleasant to drive, but I think buyers should budget for preventive maintenance instead of assuming every repair will be inexpensive. If maintained properly, it rewards you with excellent efficiency.

## Review 6 — Government employee, Peshawar

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I've owned my Hyundai Verna for nearly six years, and it has never failed to start or left me stranded. Most of my driving is within Peshawar, with occasional trips to Islamabad.

The engine remains surprisingly refined for a diesel, and fuel economy is consistently better than the petrol sedans owned by friends. The air conditioning is powerful enough for extremely hot summer afternoons, and cabin comfort remains excellent.

Routine servicing has never been particularly expensive. I simply follow recommended oil changes and replace filters on time. I believe many diesel problems result from neglected maintenance rather than poor engineering. Overall, ownership has been very satisfying.

## Review 7 — Long-term owner, Faisalabad

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

After seven years with this car, I can confidently say its biggest strengths are reliability and highway comfort. The suspension absorbs uneven roads well, and long-distance driving never feels tiring.

The diesel engine performs best once warmed up and delivers plenty of pulling power without frequent gear changes. Even with today's fuel prices, operating costs remain reasonable because of the excellent mileage.

The only drawback is resale. Since imported Hyundai sedans aren't as common as Toyota or Honda models in Pakistan, finding the right buyer can take longer. That said, I purchased the car to keep it, so resale hasn't been a major concern for me.

## Review 10 — Used imported purchase, Hyderabad

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

Buying an older imported Verna taught me one important lesson: maintenance history matters more than mileage. Shortly after purchase, I had to replace suspension components and repair the turbo because the previous owner had neglected servicing.

Since completing those repairs, the vehicle has become reliable and enjoyable to drive. Fuel economy remains excellent, and the diesel engine feels much stronger than naturally aspirated petrol alternatives.

I wouldn't discourage anyone from buying one, but I strongly recommend arranging a comprehensive inspection before finalizing the purchase. A well-maintained Verna is a very different experience from a neglected one.

## Review 11 — Daily city use, Sialkot

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

The Verna has become my preferred city car despite being designed as a sedan for mixed driving. Steering is light, visibility is good, and the diesel engine makes city driving surprisingly relaxed because it doesn't require high engine speeds.

My monthly fuel expenses have decreased noticeably since switching from my previous petrol vehicle. Regular servicing hasn't presented any unusual challenges, and my workshop has been able to source maintenance parts without difficulty.

Interior quality has also held up well despite the car's age. There are a few small rattles now, but nothing unexpected. Overall, ownership has been consistently positive.

## Review 12 — Engineer, Abbottabad

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I frequently travel through hilly areas around Abbottabad, and the Verna's diesel torque makes climbing steep roads much easier than the petrol hatchback I previously owned. The engine never feels underpowered, even with passengers and luggage.

Ride comfort is excellent, and highway stability inspires confidence. Fuel economy remains impressive despite mixed driving conditions.

## Review 13 — Second owner, 4 years ownership — Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I purchased my Verna from another owner who had maintained it meticulously, and I believe that has made all the difference. Over four years, I've only replaced normal wear items like brake pads, tires, filters, and the battery.

The CRDi engine continues to feel smooth and responsive, while the fuel economy consistently helps offset Pakistan's increasing diesel costs. The SX trim still feels generously equipped compared to many sedans from the same period.

Although resale demand isn't as strong as Toyota or Honda models, I haven't regretted my purchase for a single day. The combination of comfort, fuel efficiency, reliability, and driving refinement makes the Hyundai Verna 1.6 SX Diesel one of the most underrated imported sedans available in Pakistan's used car market.
