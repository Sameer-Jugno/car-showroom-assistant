# Customer Reviews & Ownership Testimonials — Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT S (Pakistan Used Car Market)

---

## Review 1 — Family SUV, frequent road trips — Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

We've owned our Hyundai Creta 1.6 VTVT S for almost four years, and it has become our preferred vehicle for both city driving and family road trips. We've driven from Lahore to Murree, Islamabad, and even Swat without any major issues. The elevated driving position gives excellent visibility, and my wife feels much more confident driving it than our previous sedan.

The naturally aspirated petrol engine is smooth and quiet. It's not a performance SUV, but it feels refined and predictable. On highways, I usually get around 15–16 km/l, while city driving averages around 11–13 km/l depending on traffic.

Routine maintenance has been straightforward. We use a trusted Hyundai specialist, and service parts like filters and brake pads are easy enough to find. My only complaint is that body parts can take time to source if they're not already available locally. Overall, it's been a dependable family SUV that has comfortably met our expectations.

## Review 2 — Daily office commute, Karachi

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I drive nearly 50 kilometers every day across Karachi, and the Creta has handled city traffic surprisingly well. The seating position is excellent, making it much easier to judge surrounding traffic compared to lower sedans. The suspension also absorbs potholes and broken roads much better than my previous Honda City.

Fuel economy is decent for an SUV, although rising petrol prices mean I pay noticeably more than friends with smaller hatchbacks. Still, I knew that before buying it.

Maintenance hasn't been difficult. Oil changes and routine servicing are reasonably priced, but genuine Hyundai parts sometimes need to be ordered. The interior still feels modern, and everything continues to function properly after several years. I would definitely recommend it to someone looking for a practical compact SUV.

## Review 3 — First imported SUV, Islamabad

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

This was my first imported SUV, and I was initially worried about maintenance and spare parts. Thankfully, those concerns turned out to be smaller than expected. Routine service items are readily available through Hyundai workshops and independent importers.

The cabin is spacious enough for my family of five, and the rear seats are comfortable even during long journeys. My children especially enjoy the higher seating position because they can see outside more easily.

Performance is smooth rather than exciting. The 1.6 VTVT engine delivers power progressively, and I appreciate that it's naturally aspirated because maintenance feels simpler than turbocharged alternatives. If someone wants a reliable family crossover without unnecessary complexity, this is a solid option.

## Review 4 — Business owner, Faisalabad

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I travel extensively throughout Punjab for work, so comfort was my biggest priority. The Creta has been excellent for long-distance driving. The seats remain supportive after several hours, and road noise is lower than I expected from an SUV in this price range.

Fuel economy on highways is respectable, especially if you maintain a steady speed. Around Faisalabad city, consumption naturally increases because of traffic.

Maintenance costs are slightly higher than locally assembled cars, but nothing unreasonable. I did have to wait about ten days for a replacement mirror assembly after a parking accident, which was frustrating. Apart from that, ownership has been smooth and dependable.

## Review 5 — Second owner, Rawalpindi

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

My experience has been mixed, although I don't blame the vehicle itself. The previous owner had skipped several maintenance items, and within the first year I had to replace suspension bushes, engine mounts, and a few cooling system components.

After investing in those repairs, the Creta became much more reliable. The engine feels smooth, the steering is light, and visibility is excellent. It has become an enjoyable everyday SUV.

I would strongly recommend getting a detailed inspection before buying any used imported vehicle. A well-maintained Creta is a completely different experience from one that has been neglected.

## Review 10 — Small business use, Karachi

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

I use the Creta for both family transportation and client meetings. It presents a professional appearance and feels much more comfortable than many compact sedans.

However, ownership isn't as inexpensive as some locally assembled vehicles. Genuine Hyundai parts can cost more, and certain imported components require waiting. Fuel consumption in heavy Karachi traffic also increases noticeably when the air conditioning runs continuously during summer.

Despite these complaints, the vehicle itself remains dependable. If someone understands the slightly higher ownership costs associated with imported SUVs, they'll probably be satisfied.

## Review 11 — Long-term owner, Sialkot

## Review 13 — Second owner, 4 years ownership — Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I bought my Hyundai Creta after comparing several imported compact SUVs, and it has exceeded my expectations. The cabin feels spacious, visibility is outstanding, and overall refinement is impressive for a vehicle in this segment.

The 1.6 VTVT engine isn't designed for aggressive driving, but it's smooth, reliable, and easy to maintain. I consistently achieve around 12 km/l in Lahore traffic and approximately 16 km/l on motorway trips when driving sensibly.

Servicing has been predictable, and independent Hyundai specialists have handled every maintenance requirement without difficulty. Although imported body parts occasionally require advance ordering, mechanical reliability has been excellent throughout my ownership. For families looking for a comfortable, dependable, and practical compact SUV in Pakistan's used imported market, I believe the Hyundai Creta remains one of the strongest options available.
