# Customer Reviews & Ownership Testimonials — Maruti Suzuki 800 AC (Pakistan Used Car Market)

---

## Review 1 — Owned for 4 years, daily office commute — Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I bought my Maruti Suzuki 800 AC as my first car because my budget was limited, and honestly it has exceeded my expectations. I drive around 35–40 km every day between Township and Gulberg in Lahore, and the car has been extremely economical. With petrol prices always changing in Pakistan, having a small engine really helps. I usually get around 15–17 km/l in city traffic if I drive calmly.

Maintenance has been surprisingly affordable. My local Suzuki mechanic understands the car well because many parts are similar to the Mehran. Oil changes, filters, and brake servicing don't put much pressure on my monthly budget. The AC works well after getting the compressor serviced, although the engine definitely feels less powerful when it's running.

The interior is basic and road noise is noticeable, but I didn't buy this car for luxury. I wanted something dependable and inexpensive to own, and that's exactly what it has been.

## Review 2 — First family car, owned for 2 years — Rawalpindi

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

This is our family's first four-wheel vehicle after years of using motorcycles. The Maruti 800 AC has been reliable for school runs, grocery shopping, and visiting relatives around Rawalpindi and Islamabad. Parking is incredibly easy, even in crowded markets.

Fuel consumption is one of its biggest strengths. Compared to larger cars, the monthly petrol bill is noticeably lower. Spare parts are mostly easy to find, although I had some difficulty locating an original headlight assembly because my car is an imported model.

The cabin isn't very spacious when five adults are inside, and highway trips require patience because the small engine struggles during overtaking. Still, for normal city use, it performs exactly as expected. If someone is buying their first car with a limited budget, I think it's a sensible option.

## Review 3 — University student, owned for 18 months — Faisalabad

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

As a university student, affordability was my biggest concern. I bought this car mainly because I wanted something cheaper to maintain than many other hatchbacks. It has been a fantastic learning car. The clutch is light, visibility is excellent, and the compact size makes driving through Faisalabad traffic much less stressful.

The engine has never given me any serious issues. Routine maintenance is inexpensive, and my mechanic usually completes servicing within a couple of hours. I appreciate that many mechanical parts are interchangeable with Suzuki Mehran components.

The only downside is cabin comfort. Long drives become tiring because of road noise and basic seating. The AC cools reasonably well, but acceleration becomes slower when it's turned on. Even with those compromises, I don't regret buying it because the running costs remain extremely low.

## Review 4 — Owned for 6 years, small business owner — Karachi

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I use my Maruti 800 AC for visiting customers across Karachi every day. It has covered well over 100,000 kilometers during my ownership. Mechanically, the car has been dependable as long as servicing is done on time. Engine oil, filters, and coolant changes make a noticeable difference.

Fuel economy has been very good despite Karachi's stop-and-go traffic. Parking is effortless compared to larger sedans or SUVs. The turning radius is also excellent.

However, the body feels lightweight, and on the Super Highway during windy conditions, the car doesn't inspire much confidence. Safety is another concern because it's an older design without modern features. Still, if your priority is minimizing expenses rather than driving luxury, it remains one of the cheapest cars to own.

## Review 5 — Second owner, weekend and city use — Multan

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

My experience has been mixed. The car is definitely economical and easy to repair, but buying an older imported vehicle means previous maintenance matters a lot. Shortly after purchasing mine, I had to replace suspension bushes, the radiator, and the AC compressor.

Once these issues were fixed, the car became much more reliable. Fuel economy remains impressive, usually around 16 km/l with careful driving. Spare mechanical parts haven't been difficult to find because many components overlap with Suzuki vehicles available locally.

What I don't like is the lack of refinement. The doors feel light, insulation is minimal, and highway driving above 90 km/h becomes noisy. It's a practical budget car rather than an enjoyable one. I would advise anyone considering one to spend money on a proper inspection before buying.

## Review 6 — Retired government employee — Peshawar

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

After retirement, I wanted a simple vehicle with low running costs, and the Maruti Suzuki 800 AC has served that purpose well. I mostly drive within Peshawar for shopping, bank visits, and family errands. The small engine is economical, and maintenance costs fit comfortably within my pension.

One thing I appreciate is that local mechanics are familiar with the engine. I rarely have to visit expensive workshops. Most repairs are straightforward and completed quickly.

The seats are comfortable enough for short trips, although younger passengers sometimes complain about the limited rear space. The AC works adequately in summer after regular servicing. Considering what I paid for the vehicle, I believe it offers excellent value for someone who simply needs dependable transportation.

## Review 7 — Daily commuter, Islamabad — Owned for 5 years

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

The biggest advantage of this car is ownership cost. Everything from insurance to servicing is affordable compared with newer imported vehicles. I travel daily between sectors in Islamabad, and the fuel bill stays reasonable even when petrol prices increase.

The gearbox remains smooth despite the car's age, and the engine starts reliably every morning. I've replaced only normal wear-and-tear items during my ownership.

The negatives are mostly related to age. Interior plastics have become brittle, finding original trim pieces isn't always easy, and cabin noise is noticeable. I also wish the braking performance felt stronger. Despite these complaints, I would still recommend it to buyers who prioritize affordability over modern features.

## Review 8 — Owned for 3 years, school teacher — Gujranwala

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

I purchased this car after selling my motorcycle because I wanted something safer during the rainy season. It has been extremely dependable. Every morning it starts without hesitation, and I haven't experienced any major breakdowns.

Maintenance costs remain low because my mechanic sources compatible Suzuki parts easily. Fuel efficiency has also been excellent, especially during my daily commute to school. I usually fill the tank once and drive for many days before needing another refill.

## Review 9 — Bought as a learner's car — Sialkot

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I specifically bought the Maruti 800 AC to learn manual driving before upgrading to a newer vehicle. It turned out to be a great decision. The visibility is excellent, the controls are simple, and parking is stress-free.

Because the car is mechanically uncomplicated, I also learned basic maintenance like changing filters and checking fluids myself. That has saved money and given me more confidence as a driver.

Performance is modest, especially with the AC switched on, but I never expected a sports car. It comfortably handles city driving. If you plan to drive mostly on highways with frequent overtaking, you might want something more powerful. For beginners, though, it's an excellent platform.

## Review 10 — Long-term owner, 8 years — Hyderabad

** Rating: (5/5)**

I've owned my Maruti Suzuki 800 AC longer than any other vehicle I've had. During these eight years, the car has required only routine maintenance and a few age-related repairs. The engine has remained reliable despite covering substantial mileage.

Fuel economy is still one of the strongest reasons to keep it. Even when petrol prices increased significantly, I never seriously considered selling it because operating costs remained manageable.

Resale demand has also surprised me. Whenever I considered upgrading, several interested buyers contacted me quickly because affordable cars are always in demand. Although newer vehicles offer far better comfort and safety, the Maruti continues to make financial sense for my family's needs.

## Review 11 — Imported vehicle owner, Lahore

**Rating: ★★★☆☆ (3/5)**

The imported build quality is slightly better than some locally assembled cars of a similar age, but ownership hasn't been completely trouble-free. Certain body parts and interior trim pieces took time to locate after a minor accident. Mechanical parts were easy enough to source, but cosmetic components were not.

The engine itself has been dependable, and fuel consumption remains excellent. I appreciate the simple design because almost every neighborhood workshop understands how to repair it.

If you're expecting modern refinement, you'll probably be disappointed. Wind noise, vibrations, and basic equipment remind you that this is an older budget hatchback. My advice is to buy one only if your expectations match what the car was originally designed to offer.

## Review 12 — Small-town ownership, Bahawalpur

**Rating: ★★★★★ (5/5)**

Living in a smaller city, I was worried about finding spare parts, but that hasn't been a major issue. Most service items are readily available, and mechanics familiar with Suzuki vehicles can usually repair anything without difficulty.

The car handles daily errands very well, including trips to nearby villages on paved roads. The higher fuel efficiency compared with many older sedans helps reduce monthly expenses, which has become increasingly important.

Comfort is basic, and I avoid taking five adults because the cabin becomes cramped. Other than that, ownership has been pleasant. For someone who values reliability, affordability, and simplicity above luxury, I think the Maruti Suzuki 800 AC remains an excellent budget choice.

## Review 13 — Ride-hailing and personal use — Lahore

**Rating: ★★★★☆ (4/5)**

I initially purchased this car for occasional ride-hailing work alongside my regular job. While it's not the most spacious option for passengers, its low fuel consumption made it economical to operate. The engine has proven dependable, and routine servicing rarely costs much compared with larger vehicles.

Passengers sometimes comment that the cabin feels dated and lacks modern conveniences, which is understandable given the car's age. On very hot summer afternoons, the AC takes a little longer to cool the cabin, especially when the car has been parked in direct sunlight.

Overall, the biggest strengths are affordability, simple mechanics, and inexpensive maintenance. The biggest weaknesses are limited performance, basic comfort, and older safety standards. For buyers with realistic expectations and a limited budget, it remains one of the most practical used hatchbacks available.
